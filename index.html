Subquadratic attention makes ultra‑long context practical

Latent Sparse Attention: Towards Subquadratic Ultra-Long Context Attention

Ales Kubicek, Andrea Caciolai, Liangzu Peng · cs.AI · 9/10 signal

Swap standard self‑attention for latent sparse attention to handle >20k token contexts on a single GPU.

Long‑horizon tasks demand context lengths that quadratic attention cannot sustain. This work introduces Latent Sparse Attention, reducing complexity to near‑linear by projecting queries into a latent space and sparsifying attention patterns. Practitioners gain the ability to feed models tens of thousands of tokens without exploding memory, unlocking richer scientific and code‑completion workflows.
