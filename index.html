Paper of the Week

Turn small model failures into stronger reasoning at inference time

Yufan Wu, Yinghui He, Zhengyi Hu · cs.CL

LLMs still stumble on nuanced reasoning tasks, and inference‑time tricks rarely address systematic failure patterns.

CritICL observes that small‑model errors expose latent reasoning pathways; by feeding these failure modes back into the same model at inference, it induces a weak‑to‑strong generalization boost without retraining.

Practitioners can plug the method into existing pipelines, gain immediate accuracy lifts on benchmarks like GSM‑8K, and avoid the compute cost of full fine‑tuning.

Key Takeaway

Wrap your model with CritICL to harvest failure signals for on‑fly improvement.
