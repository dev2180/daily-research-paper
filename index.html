Dual encoders finally speak the same language via weight-space tricks

TowerBridge: Bridging the Dual-Encoder Modality Gap via Weight-Space Programming

Hani Alomari, Md Atabuzzaman, Chris Thomas · cs.CV · 8/10 signal

Apply TowerBridge’s weight-space mapping to your dual-encoder models to close the modality gap with minimal compute.

Autonomous systems still suffer from a modality gap: image and text encoders occupy disjoint regions in CLIP’s shared space, limiting cross-modal reasoning. TowerBridge introduces weight-space programming to align the two towers without retraining the whole model, preserving performance while drastically reducing divergence. Practitioners gain a drop-in upgrade for existing CLIP pipelines, enabling more reliable retrieval and zero-shot classification across modalities.
