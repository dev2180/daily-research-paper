Real-time head avatars finally get photorealistic relighting

LuxAvatar: Real-Time Relightable Head Avatars via Causal Neural Shading of 3D Intrinsic Proxies

Yipengjing Sun, Chenyang Wang, Boyao Zhou · cs.CV · 9/10 signal

Integrate LuxAvatar's shading network to replace costly offline relighting in real‑time avatar systems.

Video‑driven avatar pipelines still struggle with consistent lighting across poses. LuxAvatar tackles this by learning a causal neural shading model on a 3D intrinsic proxy, enabling per‑pixel relighting at inference speed. The authors decouple geometry, albedo, and shading, then train a feed‑forward network to predict shading under arbitrary illumination. Practitioners gain a drop‑in module that turns a single portrait into a controllable, relightable avatar without costly multi‑view capture or offline rendering pipelines.
