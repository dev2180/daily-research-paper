Tokenisation hardness proves limits for practical NLP pipelines

Tokenisation is not in APX

Violeta Kastreva, Philip Whittington, Dennis Komm · cs.CL · 8/10 signal

Treat tokenisation as a heuristic step; budget for its error when evaluating model pipelines.

Current tokenisation heuristics assume a tractable approximation to an optimal segmentation. The authors show, under P≠NP, that no polynomial‑time algorithm can guarantee a constant‑factor approximation to the optimal tokeniser objective. The proof reduces from classic APX‑hard problems, establishing a theoretical barrier for any deterministic, efficient tokeniser. Practitioners must accept sub‑optimality or rely on domain‑specific shortcuts, reshaping expectations for downstream model performance.
