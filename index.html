Unified 3D‑4D reasoning bridges static and dynamic spatial intelligence

DynThinker: Unifying Static 3D and Dynamic 4D Spatial Reasoning

Qihang Cao, Haoyuan Li, Zihan Guo · cs.CV · 9/10 signal

Adopt a single transformer with spatiotemporal positional encodings to replace separate 3D and 4D models.

The paper tackles the fragmentation between static 3D scene understanding and dynamic 4D motion prediction, which hampers general spatial AI. It introduces DynThinker, a single transformer that jointly processes voxelized geometry and temporal flow, learning a shared latent space that respects both structure and motion. Practitioners gain a drop‑in model that can answer queries like "where will this object be in 2 seconds?" without stitching separate pipelines, cutting engineering overhead and latency.

Key insight: a cross‑modal attention scheme that treats time as an extra spatial dimension, reinforced by a geometry‑aware positional encoding. This design lets diffusion‑style pretraining transfer seamlessly to spatiotemporal tasks. The authors also release a benchmark spanning static reconstruction and dynamic prediction, showing state‑of‑the‑art results with half the parameters of prior dual‑model setups.

Why it matters: real‑world robotics and AR/VR systems need to reason about moving objects in context. DynThinker’s unified representation reduces model‑maintenance costs and improves inference speed, directly accelerating product development cycles.
