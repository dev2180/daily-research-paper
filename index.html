Trajectory-free SDE simulation shatters molecular dynamics bottlenecks

Kolmogorov-Chapman Maps: Trajectory-Free Learning for Simulating Molecular Dynamics

Majdi Hassan, Marta Skreta, Dominique Beaini · cs.LG · 9/10 signal

Replace explicit SDE integrators with learned transition kernels for faster, scalable simulations.

Molecular dynamics relies on numerically integrating stochastic differential equations, which is costly and error‑prone. The authors introduce Kolmogorov‑Chapman maps that learn the transition operator directly, bypassing explicit trajectory generation. Practitioners gain orders‑of‑magnitude speedups while preserving statistical fidelity, enabling rapid prototyping of force fields and real‑time inference in chemistry and biology.
