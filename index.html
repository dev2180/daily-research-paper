LLM‑guided evolution discovers solutions beyond human intuition

SwarmEvolve: Latent Agent Swarms for Evolutionary Discovery and Optimization

Qile Jiang, George Em Karniadakis · cs.AI · 9/10 signal

Wrap your existing LLM code‑generation pipeline in SwarmEvolve’s evolutionary loop to boost solution quality.

SwarmEvolve tackles the bottleneck of manual search in program synthesis by deploying a swarm of LLM agents that iteratively propose, test, and refine code. The core insight is to treat each LLM as a mutable genotype, letting evolutionary pressures surface emergent strategies that no single model would generate. Practitioners gain a drop‑in framework that scales with compute, delivering higher‑quality solutions with fewer human‑in‑the‑loop iterations.
