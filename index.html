Precision Inverse Kinematics via Reinforcement Learning Redefines Robot Control

PolicyIK: High-Precision Inverse Kinematics for Robot Arms with Reinforcement Learning

Yuhan Liu, Xinqi Liu, Zixuan Dong · cs.RO · 9/10 signal

Replace traditional IK solvers with the PolicyIK RL policy for faster, higher‑precision joint commands.

Robotic systems still struggle with high‑precision inverse kinematics, especially under visual‑language constraints. PolicyIK reframes IK as a reinforcement learning problem, training a policy that directly maps end‑effector poses to joint commands. The key insight is conditioning the RL policy on a learned latent representation of the robot’s kinematic manifold, enabling sub‑millimeter accuracy without iterative solvers. Practitioners gain a drop‑in module that eliminates costly Jacobian calculations, accelerates deployment, and integrates seamlessly with vision‑language pipelines.
