Zero-order alignment rewrites LLM preference tuning without gradients for real

A Zeroth-Order Paradigm for LLM Preference Alignment

Peter Chen, Xi Chen, Wotao Yin · cs.CL · 8/10 signal

Replace RLHF fine‑tuning with the provided zeroth‑order optimizer to achieve comparable alignment with far fewer model calls.

Aligning large language models to human preferences typically relies on costly, gradient‑based RLHF pipelines that are fragile and compute‑intensive.

The authors propose a zeroth‑order optimization framework that treats the LLM as a black box, using pairwise preference queries and Bayesian optimization to directly maximize a learned preference model.

Practitioners gain a drop‑in replacement that cuts the number of model evaluations by an order of magnitude, slashing compute budgets and simplifying deployment pipelines.
