Reward models crumble when language paraphrases diverge subtly in real

Same Trajectory, Contradictory Rewards (ROBORMBENCH): Paraphrase Fragility in Vision Language Reward Models

Wonje Jeung, Sangyeon Yoon, Hyesoo Hong · cs.RO · 8/10 signal

Add paraphrase consistency regularization when training vision‑language reward models.

Robotics pipelines now treat vision‑language models as reward functions, assuming that re‑phrasing a command won’t change the scalar feedback. In practice, that assumption breaks: the same trajectory described with minor linguistic tweaks can receive opposite rewards, leading to unsafe behavior.

The ROBORMBENCH suite quantifies this fragility by pairing each trajectory with multiple paraphrases and measuring reward variance. The authors expose systematic bias in CLIP‑based reward heads and show that even state‑of‑the‑art fine‑tuned models fail basic consistency checks.

Practitioners cannot afford hidden reward drift when deploying robots in the field. Detecting and mitigating paraphrase sensitivity now becomes a prerequisite for reliable policy learning, not a nice‑to‑have diagnostic.
