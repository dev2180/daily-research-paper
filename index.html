Mapping LM footprints to pinpoint data provenance

Elucidating the Design Space of LM Data Attribution

Steven Yu, Sheng-Yu Wang, Kimia Hamidieh · cs.CL · 8/10 signal

Integrate the proposed attribution benchmark into your fine‑tuning pipeline to expose data leakage early.

Data attribution for large language models remains opaque, hindering accountability and debugging. The paper formalizes the attribution problem and surveys the design space of tracing training data to model predictions. By introducing systematic benchmarks beyond fact‑retrieval, it reveals failure modes and trade‑offs in granularity versus scalability. Practitioners gain a concrete framework to evaluate and improve attribution pipelines, essential for compliance and model stewardship.
