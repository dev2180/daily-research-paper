Silver Rate nearly optimal: simple steps accelerate gradient descent significantly

Silver Rate Is (Almost) Optimal for Gradient Descent Acceleration

Yuhan Ye, Kaizhao Liu · math.OC · 8/10 signal

Adopt the Silver Rate schedule for any smooth convex GD task.

Gradient descent remains the workhorse for smooth convex optimization, yet practitioners often waste time tuning learning‑rate schedules. The paper asks how much acceleration is possible when the schedule is fixed in advance. The authors derive the Silver Rate, a closed‑form non‑negative stepsize sequence that approaches the theoretical optimum for any smooth convex objective.

The key insight is a spectral analysis that links the stepsize to the condition number, yielding a schedule that is provably within a small constant factor of the best possible. The result holds for any predetermined schedule, removing the need for adaptive tricks.

For engineers, the Silver Rate is a drop‑in replacement for standard constant or cosine schedules. It improves convergence speed on typical machine‑learning losses without extra hyperparameter search, making large‑scale training pipelines simpler and more predictable.
