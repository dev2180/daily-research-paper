Retriever-Free Retrieval eliminates lexical bottlenecks with decision models

Retriever-Free Retrieval: Navigating Documents with Decision Models

Chao Wan, Paul Jünger, Beixi Du · cs.AI · 9/10 signal

Replace the classic two‑stage retriever‑reader stack with a single decision model for tighter integration and faster inference.

Current RAG pipelines still depend on static lexical or embedding matches, limiting adaptability to dynamic contexts. This work proposes a decision‑model based retrieval that navigates the document space without a separate retriever, treating retrieval as a sequential decision process. Practitioners gain a unified model that can be fine‑tuned end‑to‑end, reducing latency and simplifying deployment pipelines.
