Paper of the Week
8/10 excitement

ZipDepth Brings Zero-Shot Depth Anywhere

Fabio Tosi, Luca Bartolomei, Matteo Poggi · cs.CV

Monocular depth estimation has improved with foundation models, but they are computationally demanding. ZipDepth addresses this by bringing lightweight zero-shot monocular depth estimation to any device. This breakthrough enables various applications, including robotics and autonomous vehicles, to perceive their environment more accurately without requiring large computational resources.

Key Takeaway

Practitioners can now deploy zero-shot monocular depth estimation on edge devices.
