Paper of the Week
8/10 excitement

Diffusion Models Scale Faster Now

Rogerio Guimaraes, Pietro Perona · cs.CV

Diffusion models are widely used for conditional image generation, but their inference-time scaling has been limited. The key insight of this paper is that progressive seed pruning can significantly speed up diffusion models. This breakthrough matters because it enables faster and more efficient image generation, which is crucial for many applications.

Key Takeaway

Use progressive seed pruning to scale up diffusion models
