Phase transitions reveal when random features become trainable networks

Phase Transition from Random Features to Fully Trainable Networks

Xin He, Huan Lao, Xiliang Lu · math.NA · 9/10 signal

If your model’s width exceeds the derived critical scale, switch from random features to full training for better accuracy.

Researchers confront the long‑standing trade‑off between cheap random features and fully trainable deep nets. This work maps a sharp phase transition that predicts when adding trainable parameters yields measurable gains. The authors derive a theoretical boundary and validate it across scientific computing tasks, showing when to invest compute in full training. Practitioners gain a principled rule‑of‑thumb to decide whether to rely on random feature embeddings or to allocate resources for end‑to‑end training, cutting wasted experiments.
