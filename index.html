LLMs program neural weights, unlocking on‑the‑fly model editing

WeightBench: Evaluating Language Models as Weight-Space Programmers

Aayush Marasini, Barsat Khadka, Nick Rahimi · cs.LG · 9/10 signal

Use LLM‑driven weight edits to prototype model tweaks in minutes rather than hours of fine‑tuning.

The paper tackles the opaque nature of model fine‑tuning by letting large language models directly read and modify another network's parameters. The authors demonstrate a prompt‑driven interface where the LLM proposes weight updates that achieve target behaviours without gradient descent. Practitioners gain a rapid, interpretable way to adapt models in production without costly retraining.
