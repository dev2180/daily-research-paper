ReContext revolutionizes long-context reasoning

Yanjun Zhao, Ruizhong Qiu, Tianxin Wei · cs.AI

Large language models struggle with long-context reasoning. ReContext addresses this by introducing recursive evidence replay. This innovation enables LLMs to better understand and reason over lengthy contexts, a crucial requirement for realistic applications.
